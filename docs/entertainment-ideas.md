# Entertainment idea bank — 100 original concepts

Every row is a starting concept, not a finished script. Titles are working English hooks. Each row specifies the on-screen mechanic, audio mapping and payoff; add full musical arrangement and event sheet before building. Use original assets and scores. Do not produce near-identical seed variants or misrepresent staged simulations. Prioritize the three pilot briefs before exploring other entries.
