Comment your guess: how many clacks in level 3? These blocks count the digits of pi.

Make the big block 100 times heavier and the collisions give you one more digit: 3, then 31, then 314.

Every hit you see is a note you hear. It's a real elastic collision simulation, with time stretched so the hits land on the beat.

Based on Galperin's 2003 paper "Playing Pool with Pi".
