**What's true in this video:** every heart rate is the scaling law for resting mammals, heart rate = 241 × M^-1/4 beats a minute, M in kg (Stahl 1967). Blue whale 100 t: 13.6. Elephant 5 t: 28.7. Human 70 kg: 83. Cat 4 kg: 170. Rabbit 2 kg: 203. Mouse 20 g: 641. Etruscan shrew 1.8 g (the smallest mammal by mass): 1,170, which is 19.5 beats a second. These are predictions of the law, not measurements of one animal (measured resting rates scatter around the line). Real time, no warp: each animal gets 2 bars (the shrew 8), and its heartbeats fall at the true intervals.

**Picture (3D, every effect shows the physics):** a voxel heart floats over the animal on a turntable, in a lab. Every heartbeat contracts the heart, lights it and the room (heavier hearts light it harder), and pushes one red pulse round the vessel loop in 2 s, so the gap between pulses is the gap between beats: one pulse for the whale, a chain for the mouse, no gaps left for the shrew. The whole rig shrinks with each animal (log scale) while the room keeps its size, so the room grows as the body shrinks. The only camera shake is on the whale's and the elephant's heartbeats. A white flash marks the drop, where the beats merge into one note. Outro: all seven on one straight line (log-log), then the camera pulls back out to the whale for the loop.

**Physics:** every predicted heartbeat is a chord stab (and a red pulse), snapped to the nearest 16th note (at most 42 ms; said on screen). The shrew's 19.5 Hz is faster than the 16th grid (12 a second), so its stabs merge into one held chord whose volume pulses at exactly 19.5 Hz, topped by D#4 (19.5 × 16 = 312 Hz, 4 octaves up). That chord is the drop. **Groove, not physics:** the "dem" kick tuned to each chord root (D#, B, F#, C#) in a drum and bass two-step from the first frame, a rolling reese bass on the same roots, snare, ghost snares and fills, 16th hats, a 16th pluck arpeggio, a lead hook from bar 5 (doubled an octave up in the drop), pad, riser and snare roll.

**Music:** drum and bass, 180 BPM, D# minor (D#m, B, F#, C#). Dense from 0 s: loudness per 4 s stays within 1.3 dB end to end (no empty intro). The drop lands at 16 s, halfway. Onsets sit on the 180 BPM 16th grid (low 52%, hats 83% within 15 ms of a 16th, against 36% by chance). Fine chroma: F#, D#, C#, B, A# (D# minor). Loop check: PASS.
